When a community class moves, one small change can leave several outdated notices behind. ServiceSignal helps community coordinators keep people informed.

This fictional demonstration uses a labeled guided example. Live model access is still pending.

The coordinator pastes a two-session venue change, reviews the source, and confirms the exact dates, address, room, and hours. Editing a fact disables approval until a new plan is saved.

After approval, a background worker publishes the real website and independently reads its visible facts back. Only a match becomes verified.

Residents can open the notice without an account and use its permanent QR link. Partner editing is simulated, and printed copies remain a separate manual task.

When the temporary arrangement ends, the workflow asks what happens next. It publishes the approved fallback instead of guessing that the previous venue is correct.

Our next step is a real coordinator pilot to measure time saved and information accuracy.
